## Connection Notes

The current project brief form has **name, business, project type, budget range, and brief**. It does not collect a visitor email address, phone number, or WhatsApp consent. A revised form must add those fields before an individual confirmation email or WhatsApp receipt can be sent. The backend should generate the receipt ID and timestamp, store the submission securely, email the studio receipt, and use an approved WhatsApp Business message template for any outbound WhatsApp confirmation.
